How to play

Your narration advances after the browser finishes speaking. Automatic dice are the default, and dice mode can be changed at any time. Physical-dice prompts focus the number field automatically. A DM cue signals every decision. Inspecting a room reveals concrete objects you can manipulate by name. Free text distinguishes method, target, movement and desired effect. Explore four chambers per floor; the last contains a boss, or return to the nearby village to investigate its secrets. Battlefield distance has Far, Near and Engaged zones. Cover and enemy awareness affect approaches. Most attacks connect: low rolls become glancing hits, strong rolls increase damage, and a natural 1 is the normal true miss. Criticals vary by weapon. Enemy misses can create an Opening that different classes exploit in different ways. Kills award XP; physical loot is recovered by actually searching the fallen foe. Kills award XP while corpses hold physical loot. Mana and Stamina limit stronger abilities. Mages can Center; Rogues can Catch Their Breath; Warriors can use Second Wind; and endurance tonics restore Stamina. Hidden Rogues can Sneak Advance to reposition without automatically breaking stealth. Talents can create new combat and noncombat options. Talents are labeled Shared or class-specific before purchase, and already-owned talents cannot be purchased again. Field Medicine is shared but deliberately modest and costs 3 Stamina. Death returns you to camp but costs a portion of carried gold. Gloamwick has persistent quests and a village mini-boss. Dungeon combat now includes multi-enemy encounters, target selection, encounter-based XP, AOE and crowd-control talents, and a Crypt Captain group boss. The talent language is designed to translate later into grid-based tactical combat. Portable save codes carry your adventurer between builds.
